Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

WeDo respects your privacy. This policy explains the information collected through our mobile app and services, how it is used and how it is protected.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: Your email address, display name and a salted password hash for password-based accounts.
  • Email verification: When you sign up with email, we send a time-limited code through Resend. A secure digest, expiry and attempt information are stored on the server to protect your account.
  • Sign in with Google: Google provides your user identifier, verified email address and profile name for account creation and sign-in verification. We do not receive your Google password or request access to Gmail, Drive or Calendar.
  • Sign in with Apple: Your Apple user identifier and the name and email you choose to share are used to manage your account and sessions. Hide My Email is supported.
  • Support and deletion requests: The email address and optional description you provide are sent to our support inbox to assess your request and verify account ownership.
  • User content: Links, memory photos, votes and reactions added to shared lists.
  • Device and notification data: Expo push notification tokens used to notify you of activity in shared spaces.

2. Security and sharing

Your information is not sold or shared for advertising or profiling. Communication between the app and our service is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.

Google or Apple provides authentication when you choose that sign-in method. Web support requests, including your email address and description, are sent through Resend to our Google Workspace support inbox.

3. Contact

For questions about your privacy, contact: contact@durangezer.com

Terms of Service

By downloading or using the WeDo app, you agree to these terms.

  • You must not use the app for unlawful purposes, harassment, abuse or activities that threaten the security of our service.
  • You are responsible for the links and images you add to shared spaces.
  • WeDo may update these terms or suspend accounts that violate them.

Request account and data deletion

Use this form to request account deletion even if the app is no longer on your device. Your request is emailed to support; this form does not automatically delete your account.

Once ownership is verified and deletion is completed:

  • Your registered email address and name are anonymised.
  • All active sessions are revoked.
  • Device push notification tokens are deleted from our database.
  • Content remaining in shared spaces is attributed to an anonymised account. This form does not automatically remove all shared content.
